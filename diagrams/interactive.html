BR—BW and NW—NR are hardwired links, soldered at the pickup, not switched. That is what makes position 3 fit in four poles — and what rules out intra-pickup parallel at positions 1 and 5.

Coil naming follows the Seymour Duncan code. Bridge leads are reversed (BG hot, BBk cold) because the pair is RWRP — that is what keeps the two humbuckers in phase while making both the outer and the inner coil pairs hum-cancelling. Verified on paper, not yet on a bench.
